## 👩🏻‍💻 About Me

I'm Rawda, a **Data Analyst | Business Intelligence** passionate about turning complex data into clear insights and meaningful business decisions.

I enjoy exploring data, uncovering patterns, and finding the signals hidden within the numbers. From messy datasets to interactive dashboards, I focus on transforming information into something **clear, useful, and actionable**.

But what truly draws me to data is the **curiosity behind it** — the process of looking at something that seems like just a collection of numbers and asking:

> *"What is this really telling me?"*

### 💡 My Mindset

> **Don't just look at the numbers — understand the story behind them.**

For me, every dataset starts with a question.
The goal isn't simply to find **what happened**, but to understand **why it happened**, uncover what the data is telling us, and turn those insights into better decisions.

> **Behind every number is a story. Behind every insight is a decision.**

I'm constantly exploring new ways to analyze, visualize, and communicate data. I believe the best analysis is not the one with the most charts, but the one that creates **clarity, sparks curiosity, and makes the right story impossible to ignore.**
